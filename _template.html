Closed for food safety violations.

Gıda güvenliği ihlalleri nedeniyle kapatılmıştır.

Old Town Terrace has been shut down by order of the food safety authorities of the Republic of Türkiye. It is not open to the public and is not serving food.

Old Town Terrace has been ordered to cease operations by the competent food safety authority of the Republic of Türkiye. The closure follows an official inspection that found violations of food safety and hygiene regulations at the premises.

The establishment may not prepare, serve, sell or deliver food until the closure order is lifted by that authority.

What this means

  • The premises are closed. No guests are being received.

  • Online ordering, delivery-platform listings and booking channels have been withdrawn.
  • Operations may resume only with the written authorisation of the issuing authority.

Questions and complaints

Questions concerning this decision should be directed to the issuing authority named above. Existing reservations and payments: [[CONTACT]].

Food safety concerns in Türkiye may be reported to the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry food line, ALO 174.

Türkçe

Gıda güvenliği ihlalleri nedeniyle kapatılmıştır.

Old Town Terrace, Türkiye Cumhuriyeti yetkili gıda denetim makamının kararıyla kapatılmıştır. İşletme müşteriye kapalıdır ve gıda servisi yapmamaktadır.

Old Town Terrace, resmî denetimde gıda güvenliği ve hijyen mevzuatına aykırılık tespit edilmesi üzerine, Türkiye Cumhuriyeti yetkili gıda denetim makamının kararıyla faaliyetten men edilmiştir.

İşletme, kapatma kararı yetkili makam tarafından kaldırılıncaya kadar gıda hazırlayamaz, servis edemez, satamaz ve teslimat yapamaz.
